Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

1. Information We Collect

Think and Grow Rich ("we," "our," or "us") collects the following information to provide you with a better reading and learning experience:

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Reading Progress: Your last read chapter and page position to allow you to resume easily.
  • Audio Preferences: Your playback settings and last listened chapter.
  • Display Preferences: Your font size, zoom levels, and theme settings (light/dark mode).
  • Downloaded Content: Information about which chapters you have downloaded for offline access.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, and app version to ensure technical compatibility.
  • Ad Data: Google AdMob collects anonymous identifiers and ad interaction data to show relevant advertisements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Maintain your reading continuity across sessions.
  • Provide a personalized educational experience.
  • Display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Analyze app usage to improve performance and features.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal information. We only share anonymous data with:

  • Google AdMob: For advertising services (strictly anonymous data).
  • Service Providers: For app analytics and performance monitoring.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with data, please contact us immediately.
